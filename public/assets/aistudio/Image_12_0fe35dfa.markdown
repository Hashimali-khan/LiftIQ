## Brand & Style

The design system projects raw kinetic energy, elite discipline, and biomechanical precision. Built specifically for high-performance athletes, hybrid fitness competitors, and data-driven conditioning, the interface evokes the intense focus of a low-lit performance training lab. The emotional response is calibrated between relentless motivation and absolute clarity: zero friction, zero bloat, pure momentum.

The visual style unites **Technical Glassmorphism** with **High-Contrast Athletic Precision**. Deep, light-absorbing obsidian foundations allow hyper-saturated thermal energy accents—neon electric orange and electric lime—to command attention. Structural cards use translucent obsidian surfaces with precision-milled hairline outlines, soft depth blurs, and radial illumination rings that signal metabolic output, split targets, and physical strain.

## Colors

The palette operates on high-efficiency contrast against pitch-black OLED surfaces.

- **Primary (`#F97316` / Energy Orange):** Drives immediate exertion indicators, active workout states, cardiovascular heart rate zones (Zone 4/5), and primary call-to-action touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#84CC16` / Kinetic Lime):** Represents efficiency, active recovery, readiness benchmarks, cadence targets, and successful completion states.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` / Cryo Cyan):** Accents biomechanical stats, VO2 max telemetry, hydration metrics, and secondary split data.
- **Neutral Base (`#09090B` / Obsidian Zinc-950):** The deep core foundation, complemented by `#18181B` (Zinc-900) for structural depth, `#27272A` (Zinc-800) for tactile hairline boundaries, and `#FAFAFA` for pure athletic legibility.

Color usage follows strict functional telemetry rules: gradients blend linear angles from Electric Orange to Kinetic Lime (`135deg, #F97316 0%, #84CC16 100%`) only on active output milestones, live interval progress meters, and dynamic biometric badges. Never use decorative color where data clarity is required.

## Typography

Typography pairs mechanical telemetry with rapid visual processing during rapid movement and high heart rates.

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Engineered with geometric letterforms and technical character. Used for all primary workout titles, milestone alerts, and giant numerical metrics (`metric-display`). Numbers must always be styled with tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifts during live millisecond and cadence recalculations.
- **Body & Readouts (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for high legibility at micro sizes on OLED screens. Balances clean modern neutrality with wide apertures for effortless reading while in motion.
- **Labels & Micro-Badges (Space Grotesk):** All uppercase, tightened tracking, high weight. Provides instant data classification across heart rate zones, cadence, and power splits.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a mobile-first 4-column dynamic grid (shifting to 8 columns on tablet viewports and 12 columns on analytical dashboards). Margins adapt from a compact `1rem` on narrow mobile devices up to `2rem` on expanded layouts.

Grid modules follow an ultra-dense, vertical cadence with 4px grid increments:
- **Compact Telemetry Stacks:** Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) gaps to lock numerical readouts with their corresponding unit descriptors.
- **Component Padding:** Standard card interior padding is clamped to `space-base` (16px) on mobile to maximize canvas space for data plots and rings.
- **Section Breaks:** Use `space-xl` (24px) to `space-2xl` (32px) between distinct workout interval groups to prevent tap errors during high-intensity sessions.
- **Safe Tap Zones:** Touch targets across all critical workout actions maintain an absolute minimum height of 48px with `space-sm` separation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use traditional drop shadows. Instead, it is expressed through layered translucency, backplane blurs, and neon photonic glow:

- **Base Floor (Elevation 0):** Pure `#09090B` solid matte background with zero reflection.
- **Glassmorphic Cards (Elevation 1):** Translucent surface using `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.70)` with a `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), reinforced by a 1px solid border of `rgba(39, 39, 42, 0.80)`.
- **Raised Controls & Floating HUDs (Elevation 2):** Translucent obsidian `rgba(39, 39, 42, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`, a top hairline border highlight of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, and a bottom outline of `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Dynamic Energy Glow (Photonic Elevation):** Interactive and live metrics cast radial photonic auras rather than drop shadows:
  - Active Orange Glow: `0 0 24px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.25), 0 0 8px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.40)`.
  - Target Lime Glow: `0 0 24px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.25), 0 0 8px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.40)`.
- **Modals & Drawer Sheets (Elevation 3):** Obsidian zinc-900 at 95% opacity with an upper border gradient stroke (`linear-gradient(90deg, #F97316, #84CC16)` at 20% opacity) and an ambient blackout underlay `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, athletic roundedness (`roundedness: 2`). This applies a 16px (`1rem`) corner radius to standard telemetry cards and data containers, striking the ideal balance between modern aerodynamics and structural discipline.

- **Primary Cards & Containers:** 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Interactive Buttons & Pill Badges:** Full pill-shaped rounding (`9999px`) to create frictionless tap targets and distinct aerodynamic silhouettes against angular charts.
- **Micro-Badges & Telemetry Chips:** 6px corner radius to preserve legibility inside compact data matrices.
- **Metric Rings:** Circular SVG vectors (`border-radius: 50%`) with rounded stroke caps (`stroke-linecap: round`) for dynamic velocity representation.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Pill-shaped, wrapped in a high-voltage gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F97316 0%, #84CC16 100%)`). Text is obsidian `#09090B` with `font-weight: 700`. Active state triggers an internal scale compression (`transform: scale(0.97)`) with an outer photonic bloom.
- **Ghost Glass Button:** Pill-shaped, `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.6)` background, 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text in `#FAFAFA`. On hover or press, background transitions to `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.15)` with an Orange border accent.

### Chips & Micro-Badges
- **Status & Heart Rate Micro-Badges:** Ultra-compact containers (`py-0.5 px-2`, 6px border radius). Background is a 10% alpha fill of the active metric color (e.g., `rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.1)` for HR Zone 4) paired with a 1px border at 30% alpha and matching bright label text.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped, subtle Zinc-900 background with Zinc-700 outline. Selected state swaps to a solid Electric Lime glow border with text in Kinetic Lime.

### Radial Progress Rings
- Concentric, multi-metric circular arcs built with responsive SVG paths.
- Background track is muted `rgba(39, 39, 42, 0.4)`.
- Active meter utilizes multi-stop gradients matching active performance zones, capped with rounded endpoints and an illuminated leading edge drop-shadow.

### Cards (Glassmorphism Metric Units)
- Translucent Zinc-900 layer (`rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.65)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, framed with a hairline 1px border (`#27272A`).
- Inner structure features a top telemetry row (Label + Micro-Badge), middle row dominated by `metric-display` tabular numbers, and bottom row reserved for mini sparklines or delta comparisons.

### Lists & Split Breakdown Rows
- Borderless list rows separated by 1px dividers fading to transparent at the canvas margins (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #27272A 20%, #27272A 80%, transparent)`).
- Alternate row tap states pulse with a faint `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` overlay.

### Form Inputs & Adjusters
- Inset dark wells (`#09090B`) with an inset border of `#27272A`. On focus, the border turns Electric Orange with a soft `0 0 12px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.25)` outward halo.
- Value incrementers utilize oversized mechanical tap zones suited for sweaty hands and active motion.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- Toggles feature an obsidian rail with an Electric Lime indicator pill that casts an active ambient glow when engaged.
- Checkboxes use 6px rounded squares with a Zinc-800 border; checked states snap to a solid gradient fill with an obsidian checkmark icon.
